Channel IDs follow your confirmed press test: visible sensor 8 uses channel 9, visible sensor 10 uses channel 11, and visible sensor 11 uses channel 8. Visible sensor 9 still uses channel 10. Labels and positions are unchanged. Geometry, paths, clipping and blur are preserved exactly. Calibration and data use electrical channel IDs S0–S15, not necessarily the visible labels. A practical calibration workflow

  1. Place the assembled sensor on a rigid support on the scale. Tare the support and insole before adding the loading disc. Ensure all applied force passes through the selected sensor.
  2. Capture the unloaded point. Apply several stable, known loads through the same disc and capture each one. The app averages one second of fresh readings.
  3. Repeat loads to check variability. Calibrate every channel separately, including the different sensor sizes. Do not reuse the old layer’s resistance-to-force values.
  4. A 5 kg scale tests only about 49 N. Higher walking loads remain outside range until measured with a suitable reference. Static calibration does not establish treadmill accuracy.
